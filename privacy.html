Privacy Policy for iCloud Photo Upload

Last updated: 11 November 2026

iCloud Photo Upload is a personal application used only by its owner to transfer photos and videos to the owner's Google Photos account.

Google data accessed

The application accesses Google Photos only to upload photos and videos and to manage media created through the application.

How data is used

Google user data is used solely to transfer the owner's photos and videos to the owner's Google Photos account.

Data storage and sharing

OAuth credentials are stored locally in the owner's rclone configuration. The application does not sell or share Google user data. Media is transmitted only to the owner's Google Photos account.

Data retention

Uploaded media remains in the owner's Google Photos account until the owner removes it. Temporary local files are managed by the owner's local backup process.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked through the Google Account third-party connections page. After access is revoked, the application can no longer access the Google Photos account unless it is authorized again.

Google API policy

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
